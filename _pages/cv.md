## Biography
<p style="text-align: justify;">
I am Mohammad Mahmudul Hasan, an Associate Professor in the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering at the University of Information Technology and Sciences (UITS), Bangladesh. I am currently working as a Researcher in the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Informatics at the University of Pardubice (UPCE), Czech Republic. I have more than 17 years of experience in teaching, research, student supervision, academic leadership, and industry-linked technical work across Bangladesh, India, Japan, Norway, and the Czech Republic. I completed my bachelor’s degree (2004–2008) and master’s degree (2008–2010) at KIIT University, India, and earned my PhD (2022–2025) from the Norwegian University of Science and Technology (NTNU), Norway. My PhD thesis was nominated for the Best Doctoral Thesis Award at NTNU in 2025. Earlier, I received the Chancellor’s Gold Medal and Founder’s Gold Medal for academic performance during my master’s studies at KIIT University in 2010, along with the Chancellor’s Merit Scholarship for academic excellence throughout my bachelor’s and master’s studies (2004–2010). In 2024, I was a Visiting Researcher at Brno University of Technology (VUT), Czech Republic. <br><br>

My current research at UPCE is linked to the Communication, Information and Detection Technologies for Control and Signalling Systems (CIDET) project, where I work on radar sensing, RF-based detection, localization, signal processing, AI-assisted classification, and communication and detection technologies. I am also associated with the research team of Dr. Tomáš Zálabský, whose work focuses on modern radar systems, radar signal processing, object detection, localization, identification, classification, antenna systems, microwave circuits, DSP/FPGA-based implementation, and GNSS-related applications. This current role extends my earlier research on wireless communication systems, 5G/6G networks, integrated sensing and communication, RF and microwave sensing, MIMO-OFDM systems, antenna-sensor platforms, federated learning, RF-vision fusion, and intelligent sensing systems. <br><br>

I have been affiliated with UITS since 2011 and currently serve as an Associate Professor while on approved leave for my research position at UPCE. During my tenure at UITS, I held several academic leadership roles, including Head of the Department (2019–2022), Director of the Information and Communication Technology Cell (2020–2022), and Advisor to the ICT Cell. I also contributed to institutional governance as a Member of the Finance Committee, Editorial Board Member, Member of the Academic Council, and Member Secretary of the Faculty. Alongside my academic career, I worked with industry as a consultant and technical trainer for Sanyo Engineering and Construction Inc. (Suntec Group, Japan) during 2018–2019. <br><br>

I have contributed to several funded research and institutional projects. These include Communication, Information and Detection Technologies for Control and Signalling Systems (CIDET) as a Researcher / Project Member at UPCE (2026–Present), Machine Learning Assisted Signal Processing for Antenna-Sensor Arrays as a Work Package Member (2024–2025), Air Quality Monitoring Exploiting Massive Multiple-Input Multiple-Output Antennas as a Work Package Member (2022–2025), the University Comprehensive Academic Management Software Implementation Project as Project Manager (2020–2022), and the Higher Education Quality Enhancement Project as a Member (2017–2018). My work combines RF measurements, wireless communication, radar-related sensing, signal processing, machine learning, laboratory validation, and practical engineering implementation.
</p>
